This Privacy Policy explains how Sakred Studio, the operating brand of 17899181 Canada Inc. Individual apps may provide an additional, app-specific privacy notice that supplements this policy.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on the app and the features you use, this may include:

  • Account information you choose to provide, such as your email address, when you create an account.
  • Content you enter into an app in order for it to work for you (for example, preferences or entries you save).
  • Basic technical and usage data, such as app version, device type, and anonymized analytics, used to keep the app stable and improve it.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our Services.
  • To personalize your experience — only with your consent.
  • To diagnose problems, improve reliability, and develop new features.
  • To communicate with you about your account or important changes.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not link your identity across our different apps without your consent.
  • Our apps do not provide medical or psychological diagnosis and are not a substitute for professional advice.

Data sharing

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our Services (such as hosting, authentication, and analytics providers), and only to the extent necessary. These providers are required to protect your information. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data security & retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, and we retain it only for as long as needed to provide the Services or as required by law. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Your choices & rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you can withdraw consent for optional personalization at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do, and significant changes will be communicated where appropriate.
